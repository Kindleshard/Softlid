# Changelog

What changed in each version of BlinkBuddy. The downloads are on the [releases page](https://github.com/Kindleshard/BlinkBuddy/releases).

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-28)

The first release.

- **Blink reminders**: a little eye that blinks with you, or soft eyelids across the screen.
- **Breaks**: look away every 20 minutes and stand up every hour, with guided eye exercises (a liquid eye to follow)
  and a stretch coach, read aloud in 21 languages if you like.
- **Screen filter**: warmer light after sunset, following sunset and sunrise where you are or Windows Night light;
  brightness below what the display allows; grayscale, sepia, e-ink and red night; invert; the display's real
  backlight over DDC/CI.
- **Tone**: calms bright areas and deepens darks, with a visibility guard that keeps dark details readable.
- **Modes and app rules**: Reading, Movie, Editing and more in one click, or a mode per app.
- **Reading strip, mini mode, focus timer**, film grain, keyboard shortcuts, and keeping the PC awake.
- **21 languages**, a Simple and an Advanced view, keyboard and screen reader support, dark title bars and menus.
- **Updates** from this repository, checked weekly (or by hand) and installed in place.
- An installer (no administrator rights; the spoken steps in one language or all) and a portable zip, for x64 and
  ARM64 PCs.
