# Privacy

BlinkBuddy has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry. It doesn't send anything about you or your PC
anywhere on its own.

## What stays on your PC

- Your settings, in `%AppData%\BlinkBuddy\settings.ini`.
- A short log of when BlinkBuddy started and stopped (`runs.log`, next to it), to help find problems. It never leaves
  the PC.

## When it goes online

Only for these, and only as described:

- **Checking for updates**: once a week (if the option is on) and when you click **Check for updates**, BlinkBuddy asks
  GitHub for the latest release of BlinkBuddy. If there is a newer one, it downloads the new `BlinkBuddy.exe` from
  GitHub. GitHub sees the request, as it would for any visit (see
  [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)).
- **DesktopLUT**: only when you choose **Download and set up** for the DesktopLUT tone engine, BlinkBuddy downloads it
  from its GitHub releases.
- **Your location**: only for the sunset-to-sunrise schedule, BlinkBuddy asks Windows where you are, a few times a day,
  and only if Windows allows it (Settings > Privacy & security > Location). The answer is used on your PC to work out
  sunset and sunrise, and kept in your settings; it isn't sent anywhere.

## When you choose to

- **Report a bug** opens a GitHub issue form in your browser with BlinkBuddy's version, your Windows version and
  BlinkBuddy's language filled in. You see all of it, and nothing is sent until you submit it yourself. Issues are
  public.
- **Support on Ko-fi** opens the Ko-fi page in your browser.
