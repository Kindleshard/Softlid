BlinkBuddy's spoken steps were made with Chatterbox Multilingual by Resemble AI
(https://github.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox), an open-source text to speech model under the MIT licence, in its own
voice. Each folder is one language: da de el en es fi fr it ja ko nl pl pt ru sv tr zh. Chatterbox marks what it
speaks with an inaudible watermark (Resemble AI's PerTh), which says the clips were made by a machine.

Chatterbox doesn't speak these four, so they were made with Piper (https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) from
these voices (https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices):
