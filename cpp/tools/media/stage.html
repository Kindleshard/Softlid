Where the river meets the sea

Tuesday · 6 min read

The tide came in slowly this morning, folding the light over the sandbars one ribbon at a time. Fishermen waited at the edge of the estuary, coffee steaming in the cold, watching the birds decide where the fish had gone.

Fresh water is lighter than salt water, so for a while the river simply rides on top of the sea, a thin brown layer drifting out past the harbour wall before the waves finally mix the two together.

By noon the wind had turned. The water went from slate to a clear, bright green, and the far shore came close enough to count the houses. It is the kind of day that makes you forget how long you have been staring at a screen.
